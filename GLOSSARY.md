# Risinghurst Wildlife

Local nature information for Risinghurst. It brings together digitised wildlife posters from 2018–2020, recent community sightings, and new posters built from both.

## Language

### Sources

**Original Poster**:
One of the A4 wildlife posters displayed locally between 2018 and 2020, now digitised from PDF.
_Avoid_: PDF, old poster, recording

**Poster Record**:
A mention of a Species on an Original Poster, dated by that poster's year and month.
_Avoid_: recording, sighting, mention

**Observation**:
A research-grade iNaturalist sighting of a Species near Risinghurst, made by a named observer at a specific time.
_Avoid_: sighting, record

### Species and notes

**Species**:
A kind of living thing, identified by its Scientific Name (e.g. _Vulpes vulpes_). Usually a true species, but can be a genus or broader group when an Original Poster was vague (e.g. Apple Tree → _Malus_). Common names are for display only.
_Avoid_: animal, plant, entry

**Scientific Name**:
The unique identity of a Species, shared across every data source.
_Avoid_: Latin name, taxon name

**Poster Name**:
The name a Species was given on an Original Poster (e.g. "Fox"). Each Poster Name maps to exactly one Scientific Name.
_Avoid_: common name (when meaning the poster's wording)

**Fragment**:
A short, hand-written note about one Species. It says when you might encounter the Species and how you'd notice it (e.g. "vocal at night in winter during breeding season").
_Avoid_: snippet, note, custom content

**Season**:
The months in which a Fragment says you might expect to encounter a Species. Not the same as the month of a Poster Record.
_Avoid_: months (when meaning expectation), breeding time

**When**:
The time of day at which a Fragment applies: day, night, dawn or dusk. Never a month.
_Avoid_: activity, active

**Sense**:
How you'd notice a Species: see, hear, smell, touch or taste.

### New posters

**New Poster**:
A printable one-page A4 web page for a given month, continuing the tradition of the Original Posters.
_Avoid_: poster (when ambiguous)

**Recent Sightings**:
The part of a New Poster listing selected Observations from the previous month.

**Senses Block**:
The part of a New Poster showing Fragments that share a Sense, When and Season, e.g. "Things you might hear at night in November".
