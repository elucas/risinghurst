Fox

Vulpes vulpes
Species of mammal

The red fox is the largest of the true foxes, and the most widely distributed member of the order Carnivora. It is present across the entire Northern Hemisphere, including most of Europe, Asia, and North America, as well as parts of North Africa. Its range has expanded alongside human settlement, with the species having been introduced into Australia, where it preys on native small and medium-sized rodents and marsupials. The red fox is listed as of Least Concern on the IUCN Red List. Because of its impact on native species, it is included on the list of the "world's 100 worst invasive species".

    Winter is the breeding season, when foxes are at their most vocal. Listen for screams and barks after dark.
