Reeves's Muntjac: Barking

Reeves's Muntjac

Muntiacus reevesi

Sometimes known as the 'barking deer'. They're the smallest species in the UK, but you wouldn't know that from the amount of noise they make.

Is it breeding season for Muntjac? No - they breed all year round. This might be part of why they're now classed as an Invasive Species. They bark to establish territory and warn of predators.
