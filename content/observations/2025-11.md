<div class="subheader"><em>Harmonia axyridis</em> · Insecta · 1 observation</div>
<div class="description">Species of beetle</div>
<p><i><b>Harmonia axyridis</b></i> is a lady beetle, ladybug, or ladybird species that is most commonly known as the <b>harlequin</b>, <b>Asian</b>, or <b>multicolored Asian lady beetle</b>. This is one of the most variable lady beetle species in the world, with an exceptionally wide range of color forms. It is native to eastern Asia, and has been artificially introduced to North America and Europe to control aphids and scale insects. It is now common, well known, and spreading in those regions, and has also become established in Africa and widely across South America.</p>
