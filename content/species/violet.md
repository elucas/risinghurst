<p><i><b>Viola odorata</b></i> is a species of flowering plant in the family Violaceae. The small hardy herbaceous perennial is commonly known as <b>wood violet</b>, <b>sweet violet</b>, <b>English violet</b>, <b>common violet</b>, <b>florist's violet</b>, or <b>garden violet</b>.</p>
