<p><i><b>Arrhenatherum elatius</b></i> is a species of flowering plant in the grass family Poaceae, commonly known as <b>false oat-grass</b>, and also <b>bulbous oat grass</b>, <b>tall oat-grass</b>, <b>tall meadow oat</b>, <b>onion couch</b> and <b>tuber oat-grass</b>. It is native throughout Europe, and also western and southwestern Asia, and northwestern Africa. This tufted grass is sometimes used as an ornamental grass and is sometimes marketed as "cat grass".</p>
