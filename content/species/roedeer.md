<p>The <b>roe deer</b>, also known as the <b>roe</b>, <b>western roe deer</b>, or <b>European roe</b>, is a species of deer. The male of the species is sometimes referred to as a <b>roebuck</b>. The roe is a small deer, reddish and grey-brown, and well-adapted to cold environments. The species is widespread in Europe, from the Mediterranean to Scandinavia, from Scotland to the Caucasus, and east as far as northern Iran.</p>
