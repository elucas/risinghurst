<p><i><b>Trifolium dubium</b></i>, the <b>lesser trefoil</b>, <b>suckling clover</b>, <b>little hop clover</b> or <b>lesser hop trefoil</b>, is a flowering plant in the pea and clover family Fabaceae. This species is generally accepted as the primary plant to represent the traditional Irish shamrock.</p>
