<p><i><b>Quercus cerris</b></i>, the <b>Turkey oak</b> or <b>Austrian oak</b>, is a species of oak in the beech family (Fagaceae). It is native to southern and south-eastern Europe, Asia Minor, and the Levant, but has been introduced outside its range. It is the type species of <span><i>Quercus</i> sect. <i>Cerris</i></span>, a section of the genus characterised by shoot buds surrounded by soft bristles, bristle-tipped leaf lobes, and acorns that usually mature in 18 months.</p>
