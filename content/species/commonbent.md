<p><i><b>Agrostis capillaris</b></i>, the <b>common bent</b>, <b>colonial bent</b>, or <b>browntop</b>, is a rhizomatous and stoloniferous perennial in the grass family (Poaceae). It is native to Eurasia and has been widely introduced in many parts of the world. Colonial bent grows in moist grasslands and open meadows, and can also be found in agricultural areas, roadsides, and invading disturbed areas.</p>
