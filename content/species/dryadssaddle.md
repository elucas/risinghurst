<p><i><b>Cerioporus squamosus</b></i>, synonym <i>Polyporus squamosus</i>, is a basidiomycete bracket fungus, with common names including <b>dryad's saddle</b> and <b>pheasant's back mushroom</b>. It has a widespread distribution, being found in Eurasia and North America. It causes a white rot in the heartwood of living and dead hardwood trees.</p>
