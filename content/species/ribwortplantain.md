<p><i><b>Plantago lanceolata</b></i> is a species of flowering plant in the plantain family Plantaginaceae. It is known by the common names <b>ribwort plantain</b>, <b>narrowleaf plantain</b>, <b>English plantain</b>, <b>ribleaf</b>, <b>lamb's tongue</b>, and <b>buckhorn</b>. It is a common weed on cultivated or disturbed land.</p>
