<p><i><b>Arum maculatum</b></i>, commonly known as <b>cuckoopint</b>, <b>jack-in-the-pulpit</b> and other names, is a woodland flowering plant species in the family Araceae. It is native across most of Europe, as well as Eastern Turkey and the Caucasus.</p>
