<p><b>Timothy</b> is a species of perennial grass native to most of Europe and northern and western Asia. It is also known as <b>timothy-grass</b>, <b>meadow cat's-tail</b> or <b>common cat's-tail</b>. It is a member of the genus <i>Phleum</i>, which consists of 17 species of annual and perennial grasses.</p>
