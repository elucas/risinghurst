<p>The <b>European goldfinch</b>, or simply <b>goldfinch</b>, is a small passerine bird in the finch family that is native to the Palearctic zone in Europe, northern Africa, and western Asia. It has been introduced to other areas, including Australia, New Zealand, Uruguay and the United States.</p>
