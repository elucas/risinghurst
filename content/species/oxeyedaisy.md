<p><i><b>Leucanthemum vulgare</b></i>, commonly known as the <b>ox-eye daisy</b>, <b>oxeye daisy</b>, <b>dog daisy</b>, <b>marguerite</b> and other common names, is a widespread flowering plant native to Europe and the temperate regions of Asia, and an introduced plant to North America, Australia and New Zealand.</p>
