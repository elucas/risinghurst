<p><b>Beech</b> is a genus of deciduous trees in the family Fagaceae, native to subtropical and temperate Eurasia and North America. There are 14 accepted species in two distinct subgenera, <i>Englerianae</i> <span style="font-size:85%">Denk &amp; G.W.Grimm</span> and <i>Fagus</i>. The subgenus <i>Englerianae</i> is found only in East Asia, distinctive for its low branches, often made up of several major trunks with yellowish bark. The better known species of subgenus <i>Fagus</i> are native to Europe, western and eastern Asia and eastern North America.</p>
