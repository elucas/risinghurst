<p><i><b>Anthocharis cardamines</b></i>, the <b>orange tip</b>, is a butterfly in the family Pieridae, which contains about 1,100 species. <i>A. cardamines</i> is mainly found throughout Europe and temperate Asia (Palearctic). The males feature wings with a signature orange pigmentation, which is the origin of <i>A. cardamines</i><span class="nowrap">'</span> common name.</p>
