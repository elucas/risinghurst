<p><i><b>Lamium album</b></i>, commonly called <b>white dead-nettle</b>, is a flowering plant in the family Lamiaceae. It is native throughout Europe and Asia, growing in a variety of habitats from open grassland to woodland, generally on moist, fertile soils.</p>
