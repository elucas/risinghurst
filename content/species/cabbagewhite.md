<p><i><b>Pieris brassicae</b></i>, the <b>large white</b>, also called <b>cabbage butterfly</b>, <b>cabbage white</b>, <b>cabbage moth</b> (erroneously), or in India the <b>large cabbage white</b>, is a butterfly in the family Pieridae. It is a close relative of the small white, <i>Pieris rapae</i>.</p>
