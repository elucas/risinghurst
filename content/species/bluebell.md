<p><i><b>Hyacinthoides non-scripta</b></i> is a bulbous perennial plant found in Atlantic areas from the north-western part of the Iberian Peninsula to the British Isles, and also frequently used as a garden plant. It is known in English as the <b>common bluebell</b>, <b>English bluebell</b> or simply <b>bluebell</b>, a name which is used in Scotland to refer to the harebell, <i>Campanula rotundifolia</i>. In spring, <i>H. non-scripta</i> produces a nodding, one-sided inflorescence of 5–12 tubular, sweet-scented violet–blue flowers, with strongly recurved tepals, and 3–6 long, linear, basal leaves.</p>
