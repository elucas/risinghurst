<p><b><i>Dactylorhiza</i> <i>fuchsii</i></b>, the <b>common spotted orchid</b>, is a species of flowering plant in the orchid family Orchidaceae. It is one of Europe's most common wild orchids, and is widespread across much of Europe, with the range extending eastward into Siberia, Mongolia and Xinjiang. The species is also reportedly naturalised in the Canadian Province of Ontario.</p>
