<p><i><b>Medicago lupulina</b></i>, commonly known as <b>black medick</b>, <b>nonesuch</b>, or <b>hop clover</b>, is a plant of dry grassland belonging to Fabaceae, the legume or clover family. It is native to Africa and Eurasia, and has been naturalized elsewhere.</p>
