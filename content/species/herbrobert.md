<p><i><b>Geranium robertianum</b></i>, commonly known as <b>herb-robert</b> or, in North America, as <b>Robert's geranium</b>, is a species of cranesbill that is widespread throughout the northern hemisphere and introduced to some countries in the southern. It is common in woods, hedges, gardens, and on waste ground, and can also be found on shingle beaches and limestone pavements. It is not rare or threatened and in some places it is considered to be invasive.</p>
