<p><i><b>Sonchus oleraceus</b></i> is a species of flowering plant in the tribe Cichorieae of the family Asteraceae, native to Europe and Western Asia. It has many common names including <b>common sowthistle</b>, <b>sow thistle</b>, <b>smooth sow thistle</b>, <b>annual sow thistle</b>, <b>puha</b>, <b>hare's colwort</b>, <b>hare's thistle</b>, <b>milky tassel</b>, <b>milk thistle</b>, and <b>soft thistle</b>.</p>
