<p><i><b>Epilobium hirsutum</b></i> is a flowering plant belonging to the willowherb genus <i>Epilobium</i> in the family Onagraceae. It is commonly known as the <b>great willowherb</b>, <b>great hairy willowherb</b> or <b>hairy willowherb</b>. Local dialectal names include <i>codlins and cream</i>, <i>apple pie</i> and <i>cherry pie</i>.</p>
