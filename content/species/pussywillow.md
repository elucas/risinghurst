<p><b>Pussy willow</b> is a name given to many of the smaller species of the genus <i>Salix</i> when their furry catkins are young in early spring. These species include :</p><ul><li>Goat willow or goat sallow, a small tree native to Northern Europe and northwest Asia.</li>
<li>Grey willow or grey sallow, a small tree native to Northern Europe.</li>
<li>American pussy willow, native to northern North America.</li></ul>
