<p><b>Polypores</b>, also called <b>bracket</b> or <b>shelf fungi,</b> are a morphological group of basidiomycete-like gilled mushrooms and hydnoid fungi that form large fruiting bodies called <b>conks</b>, which are typically woody, circular, shelf- or bracket-shaped, with pores or tubes on the underside.</p>
