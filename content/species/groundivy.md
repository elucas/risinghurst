<p><i><b>Glechoma hederacea</b></i> is an aromatic, perennial, evergreen creeper of the mint family Lamiaceae. It is commonly known as <b>ground-ivy</b>, <b>gill-over-the-ground</b>, <b>creeping charlie</b>, <b>alehoof</b>, <b>tunhoof</b>, <b>catsfoot</b>, <b>field balm</b>, and <b>run-away-robin</b>. It is also sometimes known as <b>creeping jenny</b>, but that name more commonly refers to <i>Lysimachia nummularia</i>. It is used as a salad green in many countries.</p>
