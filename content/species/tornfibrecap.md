<p><i><b>Inocybe lacera</b></i>, commonly known as the <b>torn fibrecap</b>, is a poisonous species of mushroom in the genus <i>Inocybe</i>. Its appearance is that of a typical "little brown mushroom": small, brown and indistinct. It is distinguishable by its microscopic features, particularly its long, smooth spores. There are several documented subspecies in addition to the main <i>I. lacera</i> var <i>lacera</i>, including the dwarf form <i>I. lacera</i> var. <i>subsquarrosa</i> and <i>I. lacera</i> var. <i>heterosperma</i>, found in North America.</p>
