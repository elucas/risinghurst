<p><i><b>Urtica dioica</b></i>, often known as <b>common nettle</b>, <b>burn nettle</b>, <b>stinging nettle</b>, <b>nettle leaf</b>, or just a <b>nettle</b> or <b>stinger</b>, is a herbaceous perennial flowering plant in the family Urticaceae. Originally native to Europe, much of temperate Asia and western North Africa, it is now found worldwide.</p>
