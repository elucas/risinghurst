<p>An <b>apple</b> is the round, edible fruit of an apple tree. Fruit trees of the <b>orchard</b> or <b>domestic apple</b>, the most widely grown in the genus, are cultivated worldwide. The tree originated in Central Asia, where its wild ancestor, <i>Malus sieversii</i>, is still found. Apples have been grown for thousands of years in Eurasia before they were introduced to the American continent by European colonists. Apples have cultural significance in many mythologies and religions.</p>
