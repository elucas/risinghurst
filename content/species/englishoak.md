<p><i><b>Quercus robur</b></i>, <b>pedunculate oak</b>, <b>European oak</b>, or <b>English oak</b>, is a species of flowering plant in the beech and oak family, Fagaceae. It is a large tree, native to Europe and western Asia, and is widely cultivated in other temperate regions. It grows on soils of near neutral acidity in the lowlands and is notable for its value to natural ecosystems, supporting a diversity of herbivorous insects, acorn eating mammals and birds, and fungi.</p>
