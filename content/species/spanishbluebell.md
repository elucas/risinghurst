<p><i><b>Hyacinthoides hispanica</b></i>, the <b>Spanish bluebell</b> or <b>wood hyacinth</b>, is a spring-flowering bulbous perennial native to the Iberian Peninsula. It is one of around a dozen species in the genus <i>Hyacinthoides</i>, others including the common bluebell in northwestern Europe, and the Italian bluebell further east in the Mediterranean region.</p>
