<p><i><b>Cardamine pratensis</b></i>, the <b>cuckoo flower</b>, <b>lady's smock</b>, <b>mayflower</b>, or <b>milkmaids</b>, is a flowering plant in the family Brassicaceae. It is a perennial herb native to Eurasia.</p>
