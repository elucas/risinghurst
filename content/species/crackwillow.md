<p><b><i>Salix</i> × <i>fragilis</i></b>, with the common names <b>crack willow</b> and <b>brittle willow</b>, is a hybrid species of willow native to Europe and Western Asia. It is native to riparian habitats, usually found growing beside rivers and streams, and in marshes and water meadow channels. It is a hybrid between <i>Salix euxina</i> and <i>Salix alba</i>, and is very variable, with forms linking both parents.</p>
