<p><i><b>Agaricus campestris</b></i> is a widely eaten gilled mushroom closely related to the cultivated <i>A. bisporus</i>. <i>A. campestris</i> is commonly known as the <b>field mushroom</b> or, in North America, <b>meadow mushroom</b>.</p>
