<p>Commonly known as <b>hellebores</b>, the Eurasian genus <i><b>Helleborus</b></i> consists of approximately 20 species of herbaceous or evergreen perennial flowering plants in the family Ranunculaceae, within which it gave its name to the tribe of <b>Helleboreae</b>. Many hellebore species are poisonous.</p>
