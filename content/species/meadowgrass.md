<p><i><b>Poa</b></i> is a genus of about 570 species of grasses, native to the temperate regions of both hemispheres. Common names include <b>meadow-grass</b>, <b>bluegrass</b>, <b>tussock</b>, and <b>speargrass</b>. <i>Poa</i> is Greek for 'fodder'. <i>Poa</i> are members of the subfamily Pooideae of the family Poaceae.</p>
