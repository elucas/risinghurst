<p>The inedible fungus <i><b>Daldinia concentrica</b></i> is known by several common names, including <b>King Alfred's cake</b>, <b>cramp balls</b>, and <b>coal fungus</b>. It is a common, widespread saprotrophic sac fungus, living on dead and decaying wood.</p>
