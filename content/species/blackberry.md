<p>A <b>blackberry</b> is an edible fruit produced by many species in the genus <i>Rubus</i> in the family Rosaceae, hybrids among these species within the subgenus <i>Rubus</i>, and hybrids between the subgenera <i>Rubus</i> and <i>Idaeobatus</i>. The taxonomy of blackberries has historically been confused because of hybridization and apomixis so that species have often been grouped together and called species aggregates.</p>
