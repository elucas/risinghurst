<p><i><b>Pieris rapae</b></i> is a small-to-medium-sized butterfly species of the whites-and-yellows family Pieridae. It is known in Europe as the <b>small white</b>, in North America and the United Kingdom as the <b>cabbage white</b> or <b>cabbage butterfly</b>, on several continents as the <b>small cabbage white</b>, and in New Zealand as the <b>white butterfly</b>. The butterfly is recognizable by its white color with small black dots on its wings, and it can be distinguished from <i>P. brassicae</i> by the latter's larger size and black band at the tip of the forewings.</p>
