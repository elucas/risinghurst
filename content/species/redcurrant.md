<p>The <b>redcurrant</b> or <b>red currant</b> is a member of the genus <i>Ribes</i> in the family <span><span style="font-style:normal">Grossulariaceae</span></span>. It is native to western Europe. The species is widely cultivated and has escaped into the wild in many regions.</p>
