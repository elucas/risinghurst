<p><i><b>Veronica filiformis</b></i> is a species of flowering plant in the family Plantaginaceae. It is known by many common names, including <b>slender speedwell</b>, <b>creeping speedwell</b>, <b>threadstalk speedwell</b> and <b>Whetzel weed</b>. It is native to eastern Europe and western Asia, and it is known in many other regions as an introduced species.</p>
