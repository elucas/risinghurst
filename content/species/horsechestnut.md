<p>The genus <i><b>Aesculus</b></i> is a genus of about 12 species of flowering plants in the family Sapindaceae. They are trees and shrubs, mostly native to the temperate Northern Hemisphere. The European and Asian species are known as <b>horse-chestnut</b> or <b>horse chestnut</b>, and the North American species as <b>buckeye</b>.</p>
