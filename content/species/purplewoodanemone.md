<p><i><b>Anemonoides nemorosa</b></i>, the <b>wood anemone</b>, also historically known as <b>windflower</b> because of William Turner's interpretation of its Latin name <i>anemone</i> in his 1551 <i>New Herball</i>, is an early spring flowering plant in the buttercup family Ranunculaceae, native to Europe. It is a perennial herbaceous plant growing 5–15 cm (2–6 in) tall.</p>
