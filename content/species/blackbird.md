<p>The <b>common blackbird</b> is a species of true thrush. It is also known as the <b>Eurasian blackbird</b>, or simply the <b>blackbird</b>. It breeds in Europe, western Asia, and North Africa, and has been introduced to Australia and New Zealand. It has a number of subspecies across its large range; a few former Asian subspecies are now widely treated as separate species. Depending on latitude, the common blackbird may be resident, partially migratory, or fully migratory.</p>
