<p><i><b>Rhinanthus minor</b></i>, known as <b>yellow rattle</b>, is a herbaceous wildflower in the genus <i>Rhinanthus</i> in the broomrape family. It has circumpolar distribution in Europe, Russia, western Asia, and northern North America. An annual plant, yellow rattle grows up to 10–50 centimetres (3.9–19.7 in) tall, with upright stems and opposite, simple leaves. The fruit is a dry capsule, with loose, rattling seeds.</p>
