<p>The <b>Eurasian chaffinch</b>, <b>common chaffinch</b>, or simply the <b>chaffinch</b> is a common and widespread small passerine bird – in the finch family. The male is brightly coloured with a blue-grey cap and rust-red underparts. The female is more subdued in colouring, but both sexes have two contrasting white wing bars and white sides to the tail. The male bird has a strong voice and sings from exposed perches to attract a mate.</p>
