<p>A <b>crow</b> is a bird of the genus <i>Corvus</i>, or more broadly, a synonym for all of <i>Corvus</i>. The word "crow" is used as part of the common name of many species. The related term "raven" is not linked scientifically to any certain trait but is rather a general grouping for larger-sized species of <i>Corvus</i>. The collective name for a group of crows is a "murder".</p>
