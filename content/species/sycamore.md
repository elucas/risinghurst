<p><i><b>Acer pseudoplatanus</b></i>, known as the <b>sycamore</b> in the British Isles and as the <b>sycamore maple</b> in the United States, is a species of maple native to Central Europe and Western Asia. It is a large deciduous, broad-leaved tree, tolerant of wind and coastal exposure.</p>
