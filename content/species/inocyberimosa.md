<p><i><b>Inocybe rimosa</b></i>, commonly known as <b>straw-colored fiber head</b>, is a poisonous mushroom native to Europe. Its toxic ingredient is muscarine, discovered during the 1930s. Serious poisoning can result from consuming any quantity of the mushroom.</p>
