<p><i><b>Geum urbanum</b></i>, also known as <b>wood avens</b>, <b>herb Bennet</b>, <b>colewort</b>, <b>clove root</b> and <b>St. Benedict's herb</b>, is a perennial plant in the rose family (Rosaceae), which grows in shady places in the temperate regions of Eurasia and North America.</p>
