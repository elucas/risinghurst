<p><i><b>Veronica persica</b></i> is a flowering plant in the family Plantaginaceae. Common names include <b>birdeye speedwell</b>, <b>common field-speedwell</b>, <b>Persian speedwell</b>, <b>large field speedwell</b>, <b>bird's-eye</b>, or <b>winter speedwell</b>. The most widespread species of <i>Veronica,</i> it is native to western Asia but has been introduced worldwide.</p>
