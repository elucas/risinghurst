<p><i><b>Hedera helix</b></i>, simply <b>ivy</b>, or <b>common ivy</b>, <b>English ivy</b>, <b>European ivy</b>, or <b>King's choice ivy</b>, is a species of flowering plant in the family Araliaceae. It is native to most of Europe and parts of western Asia. Ivy is a clinging evergreen vine that grows on tree trunks, walls, and fences in gardens, waste spaces, and wild habitats. Ivy is common as an ornamental plant, but escaped plants become naturalised outside its native range. Ivy has considerable cultural significance and symbolism.</p>
