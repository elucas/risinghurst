<p><i><b>Lotus corniculatus</b></i> is a flowering plant in the pea family Fabaceae. Common names include <b>common bird's-foot trefoil</b>, <b>eggs and bacon</b>, <b>birdsfoot deervetch</b>, and just <b>bird's-foot trefoil</b>. It has a wide distribution and is a favored forage for livestock.</p>
