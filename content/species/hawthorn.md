<p><i><b>Crataegus</b></i>, commonly called <b>hawthorn</b>, <b>quickthorn</b>, <b>thornapple</b>, <b>May-tree</b>, <b>whitethorn</b>, <b>Mayflower</b> or <b>hawberry</b>, is a genus of several hundred species of shrubs and trees in the family Rosaceae, native to temperate regions of the Northern Hemisphere in Europe, Asia, North Africa and North America. The name "hawthorn" was originally applied to the species native to northern Europe, especially the common hawthorn <i>C. monogyna</i>, and the unmodified name is often so used in Britain and Ireland. The name is now also applied to the entire genus and to the related Asian genus <i>Rhaphiolepis</i>.</p>
