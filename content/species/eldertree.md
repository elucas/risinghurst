<p><i><b>Sambucus nigra</b></i> is a temperate species of tree or shrub in the family Viburnaceae native to the Azores, Europe, and the Middle East. Common names include <b>elder</b>, <b>elderberry</b>, <b>black elder</b>, <b>European elder</b>, <b> European elderberry</b>, and <b>European black elderberry</b>. It grows in a variety of conditions including both wet and dry fertile soils, primarily in sunny locations. The plant is widely grown as an ornamental shrub or small tree.</p>
