<p><i><b>Daedaleopsis confragosa</b></i>, commonly known as the <b>thin-walled maze polypore</b> or the <b>blushing bracket</b>, is a species of polypore fungus in the family Polyporaceae. The species was first described from Europe in 1791 as a form of <i>Boletus</i>, and has undergone several changes of genus in its taxonomic history. It acquired its current name when Joseph Schröter transferred it to <i>Daedaleopsis</i> in 1888.</p>
