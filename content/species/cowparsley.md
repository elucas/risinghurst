<p><i><b>Anthriscus sylvestris</b></i>, known as <b>cow parsley</b>, <b>wild chervil</b>, <b>Queen Anne's lace</b> or <b>keck</b>, is a herbaceous biennial or short-lived perennial plant in the family Apiaceae (Umbelliferae). It is also sometimes called <b>mother-die</b>, a name that is also applied to <i>Crataegus monogyna</i>. It is native to Europe, western Asia and northwestern Africa. It is related to other diverse members of Apiaceae, such as parsley, carrot, hemlock and hogweed. It is often confused with <i>Daucus carota</i>, another member of the Apiaceae also known as "Queen Anne's lace" or "wild carrot".</p>
