<p>The <b>common slow worm</b> is a non-venomous species of legless lizard native to Europe. It is also called a <b>deaf adder</b>, <b>blindworm</b>, or regionally, a <b>long-cripple</b>, <b>steelworm</b>, and <b>hazelworm.</b></p>
