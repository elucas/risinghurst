<p><i><b>Taraxacum</b></i> is a genus of flowering plants in the family Asteraceae which consists of species commonly known as <b>dandelions</b>. The scientific and hobby study of the genus is known as taraxacology. The genus has a near-cosmopolitan distribution, absent only from tropical and polar areas. Two of the most common species worldwide, <i>T. officinale</i> and <i>T. erythrospermum</i>, are European species introduced into North America, where they are non native. Dandelions thrive in temperate regions and can be found in yards, gardens, sides of roads, among crops, and in many other habitats.</p>
