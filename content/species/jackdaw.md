<p><b>Jackdaws</b> are two species of bird in the genus <i><b>Coloeus</b></i> closely related to, but generally smaller than, crows and ravens (<i>Corvus</i>). They have a blackish crown, wings, and tail, with the rest of their plumage paler. The word <i>Coloeus</i> is Neo-Latin, from the Ancient Greek for jackdaws: <i><span><i lang="grc-Latn">koloiós</i></span></i> (κολοιός). They come from Asia, Europe, Africa, and Siberia.</p>
