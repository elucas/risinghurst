<p><i><b>Rumex obtusifolius</b></i>, commonly known as <b>bitter dock</b>, <b>broad-leaved dock</b>, <b>bluntleaf dock</b>, <b>dock leaf</b>, <b>dockens</b> or <b>butter dock</b>, is a perennial plant in the family Polygonaceae. It is native to Europe, but is found on all temperate continents. It is a highly invasive species in some zones, resulting from its abundant seed dispersal, adaptability to reproduce, aggressive roots, ability to tolerate extreme climates, and hardiness.</p>
