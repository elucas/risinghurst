<p><i><b>Achillea millefolium</b></i>, commonly known as <b>yarrow</b> or <b>common yarrow</b>, is a flowering plant in the family Asteraceae. Growing to 1 metre tall, it is characterized by small whitish flowers, a tall stem of fernlike leaves, and a pungent odor.</p>
