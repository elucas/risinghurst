<p><b>Walnut trees</b> are any species of tree in the plant genus <i><b>Juglans</b></i>, the type genus of the family Juglandaceae, the seeds of which are referred to as walnuts. All species are deciduous trees, 10–40 metres (33–131 ft) tall, with pinnate leaves 200–900 millimetres (7.9–35.4 in), with 5–25 leaflets; the shoots have chambered pith, a character shared with the wingnuts (<i>Pterocarya</i>), but not the hickories (<i>Carya</i>) in the same family.</p>
