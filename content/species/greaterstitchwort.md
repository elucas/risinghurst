<p><i><b>Rabelera holostea</b></i>, known as <b>greater stitchwort</b>, <b>greater starwort</b>, and <b>addersmeat</b>, is a perennial herbaceous flowering plant in the family Caryophyllaceae. It was formerly placed in the genus <i>Stellaria</i>, as <i><b>Stellaria holostea</b></i>, but was transferred to the genus <i><b>Rabelera</b></i> in 2019 based on phylogenetic analyses. It is the only species in the genus <i><b>Rabelera</b></i>. Greater stitchwort is native to Western and Central Europe, including the British Isles.</p>
