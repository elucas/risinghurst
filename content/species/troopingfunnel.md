<p><i><b>Infundibulicybe geotropa</b></i>, also known as the <b>trooping funnel</b> or <b>monk's head</b>, is a large funnel-shaped mushroom with a sturdy cream or buff colour. It grows widely in Europe and in North America in mixed woodlands, often in troops or fairy rings, one of which is over half a mile wide. Although edible, it could be confused with some poisonous species of similar colouration and size.</p>
