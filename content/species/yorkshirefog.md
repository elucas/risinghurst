<p><i><b>Holcus lanatus</b></i> is a perennial flowering plant in the grass family Poaceae. The specific epithet <i><span><i lang="la"><span class="extiw">lanatus</span></i></span></i> is Latin for 'woolly' which describes the plant's hairy texture. Common names include <b>Yorkshire fog</b>, <b>tufted grass</b>, and <b>meadow soft grass</b>. In North America, where it is an invasive species, names include <b>velvet grass</b> and <b>common velvet grass</b>.</p>
