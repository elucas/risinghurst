<p><i><b>Lolium perenne</b></i>, common name <b>perennial ryegrass</b>, <b>English ryegrass</b>, <b>winter ryegrass</b>, or <b>ray grass</b>, is a grass from the family Poaceae. It is native to Europe, Asia and northern Africa, but is widely cultivated and naturalised around the world.</p>
