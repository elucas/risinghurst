<p><i><b>Dactylis glomerata</b></i> is a species of flowering plant in the grass family Poaceae, commonly known as <b>barnyard grass</b>, <b>cocksfoot grass</b>, <b>cockspur</b>, <b>orchard grass</b>, or <b>cat grass</b> (due to its popularity for use with domestic cats). It is a cool-season perennial <span>C<sub>3</sub></span> tufted grass native throughout most of Europe, temperate Asia, and northern Africa.</p>
