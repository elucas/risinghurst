<p><i><b>Polygonia</b></i> is a genus of butterflies with a conspicuous white mark on the underside of each hindwing, hence the common name <b>comma</b>. They also have conspicuous angular notches on the outer edges of their forewings, hence the other common name anglewing butterflies. The related genus <i>Nymphalis</i> also includes some anglewing species; <i>Polygonia</i> is sometimes classified as a subgenus of <i>Nymphalis</i>.</p>
