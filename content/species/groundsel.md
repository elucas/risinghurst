<p><i><b>Senecio vulgaris</b></i>, often known by the common names <b>groundsel</b> and <b>old-man-in-the-spring</b>, is a flowering plant in the family Asteraceae. It is an annual herb, native to the Palaearctic and widely naturalised as a ruderal species in suitable disturbed habitats worldwide.</p>
