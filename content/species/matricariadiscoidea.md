<p><i><b>Matricaria discoidea</b></i>, commonly known as <b>pineappleweed</b>, <b>wild chamomile</b>, <b>disc mayweed</b>, and <b>rayless mayweed</b>, is an annual plant native to North America, northern Asia, and Europe where it grows as a common herb of fields, gardens, and roadsides. It is in the daisy family Asteraceae. When crushed, the flowers and foliage emit a strong pineapple-like aroma, giving the plant its common name.</p>
