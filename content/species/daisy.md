<p><i><b>Bellis perennis</b></i>, the <b>daisy</b>, is a European species of the family Asteraceae, often considered the archetypal species of the name <i>daisy</i>. To distinguish this species from other plants known as daisies, it is sometimes qualified or known as <b>common daisy</b>, <b>lawn daisy</b> or <b>English daisy</b>.</p>
