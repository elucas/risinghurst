## The
@Song Thrush

Another musical sound Recognisable by its repetitive nature or 4 times, loud and clear: For the secretary again,all repeated several times

The
@Robin
Sings a phrase then there's a gap then a different phrase everytime.

Slow notes tumble into rapid ones. Sings in Autumn as well.

The
@Wren
Surprisingly loud for such a small hird. Strong; dynamic over 100 notes with a twist at the end:

@Dunnock
High-pitched and squeaky like the unoiled wheels of a trolley. Doesn't have a final trill like the Wren.

## Birds

The
@Chaffinch
Cheerful rattling which gets louder and faster; drops pitch and ends emphatically. Sounds like a bowler approaching the crease with quickening footsteps and the final delivery ofthe ball

The
@Greenfinch
Wheezes and trills, its prolonged wheeze telling you its colour: greeeeeeeeeeeeen!
