### Why Yellow Rattle is important in our flower meadows
In early spring the seeds germinate and grow quickly.
As their roots develop underground they seek out the roots of plants growing nearby, especially grasses.
Once contact is made, the Yellow Rattle draws water and nutrients from them, suppressing the growth of grasses by as much as 60%.
In the resulting space, other flowers have room to grow.
