## Wild Flowers

## Flowers in the hedges and edges in this month of August

@Matricaria Discoidea
otherwise known as Pineapple Weed, Weed; Wild Chamomile May

<!-- image -->

Grows on bare and disturbled ground and well-trodden places almost everywhere.
Smells of pineapple
Good for gastrointestinal upsets, sores and fever
Flowerheads taste like pineapple
Leaves can be added to salads
