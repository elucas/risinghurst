## Butterflies &amp; Moths

Our warm and sunny summer provided disappointing numbers as The Big Butterfly Count Report says:

- Warm weather from April onwards meant flight periods were earlier this year maybe Meadow Browus and Ringlets were past their prime by August
- Stand-out winners were the Whites, large and small. Ask the gardeners!!
- Common Blue bighest sincc 2010, numbers increasing every year since 2010 51%
- Of the migrants, Red Admiral decreased substantially while the Painted Lady remained the same
- Worst discovery of all was the Small Tortoiseshell. Numbers have slumped. population siuce the 70S. This year was worst on record and worryingly no clear reason why
- One species; the Red Admiral which did very well in 2017 was one of the biggest losers this year: The cold spells in early spring may have killed overwintering Red Admirals and their Offspring and, in addition, there were low numbers migrating to the UK during spring and carly summer
