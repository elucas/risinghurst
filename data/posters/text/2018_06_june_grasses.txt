## Grasses

Lots of grasses in the flower meadow in June.
 Here are a few varieties - all good hay crops

@Cock's Foot
Purple-tinged flower heads much favoured by the Meadow brown, Hedge Brown, and Ringlet butterflies

@Rye Grass
A good belly filler for horses apparently.

@Meadow grass
The Poa family has about 500 species not all common to the British Isle and certainly not our meadow!


@Yorkshire Fog
With its velvety grey-green leaves and purple-tinged flower headers favoured by the Speckled wood butterfly.
