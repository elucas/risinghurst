## One tree in flower this month - the
@Elder Tree

- IEs said that the name elder comes from "aeld", an Anglo-Saxon word meaning fire: It seems that the hollow stems were used as bellows to blov' air into the centre of a fire
- Superstition has it that if you planted an elder by your house it would keep the devil away
- Its flowers, creamy- coloured and highly scented,are often used to make elderflower wine,cordial andtea or fried t0 make frilters:
